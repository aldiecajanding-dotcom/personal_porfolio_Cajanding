THE BUILDER / 001

Two worlds. One dream. A mind wired to understand how things move.

I’m Aldie G. Cajanding, currently taking BSIT at La Consolacion University Philippines in Malolos, Bulacan. I have always been drawn to motorcycles, mechanical systems, and the hands-on satisfaction of making something work again.

My original dream was to become a professional motorcycle mechanic. Today, I’m building my digital skills while carrying the same patience, creativity, and problem-solving mindset from the garage into every line of code.
